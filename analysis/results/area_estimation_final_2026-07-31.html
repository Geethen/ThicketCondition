How to read the estimates

The examples below use the primary ARP-preferred, nothicket-to-severe scenario. Open a term for its short definition and a live example from this report.

Area terms

Mapped area is the model’s known stratum area. Reference class is the labeller’s observed condition. Error-adjusted area redistributes mapped area using those observations. Example: Arid × mapped severe has 488,486 ha mapped, of which 435,546 ± 20,940 ha is estimated reference severe.

Accuracy and uncertainty

OA is weighted overall accuracy; UA is the reliability of a mapped class; PA is completeness of a reference class. SE measures sampling uncertainty; 95% MOE is 1.96×SE. Example: OA is 69.1%; global intact area CI is 388,095–468,264 ha.

Scenario and adjudication

Scenario controls how nothicket is treated. Adjudication resolves original ARP/SVM disagreements. The primary view counts nothicket as severe and prefers ARP labels; all alternatives remain available in the interactive charts.

Why some mapped cells receive no points. This is a targeted precision allocation, not an equal-cell allocation: Valley reference-area estimates already have lower relative uncertainty, so the limited budgets are directed to the higher-uncertainty Arid and Mesic estimates. If every EFG × mapped-class cell must receive a new measurement, impose a per-cell minimum before optimising; that will reduce the precision gain for the reference-area targets.
